The Intermediate Guide to Building Colorful, Nutrient-Packed Bowls

4 min read 126k

There's something deeply satisfying about a bowl that's as beautiful as it is nourishing. But building a bowl that checks every nutritional box while still tasting incredible? That's an art form.

For years, I treated lunch as an afterthought — a sad desk salad or a hastily assembled sandwich. It wasn't until I discovered the power of the nourish bowl that my relationship with food fundamentally changed.

The Foundation

Every great bowl starts with a solid base. I typically divide mine into two halves: grains and greens. The grains provide sustained energy, while the greens pack in micronutrients without adding excessive calories.

"Cook grains in vegetable broth instead of water. It adds depth of flavor without any extra effort."
